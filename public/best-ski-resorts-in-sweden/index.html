Skiing in Sweden: Åre

Sweden's ski resorts sit in the Scandinavian Mountains, and Åre in Jämtland is the biggest and best known. It offers cold, dry snow, a lively ski town and a real Nordic atmosphere.

Short verdict. Åre is a serious ski destination, not a small local hill. It suits skiers who want a Nordic week with good grooming, a proper town and shorter travel than a long-haul trip. It does not have Alpine altitude.

What it is like

Åre is a mountain resort with a town at its base. Åreskutan rises to 1,420 m, so the skiing is at lower altitude than the Alps, which means cold, often dry snow rather than glacier-high terrain. The resort's own figures of 46 lifts and 90 slopes put it well above Sweden's other ski areas in scale.

Getting there

You can fly to Åre/Östersund Airport, about 97 km from Åre, or take a train to the station in the centre of Åre. Stockholm is 610 km away.

Who should go

Åre if you want a Nordic ski week with a proper town, good grooming and shorter travel from Scandinavia, and you can live with lower altitude and a shorter day of light in midwinter.
The Alps if you want high-altitude terrain, huge linked areas or very long seasons.
