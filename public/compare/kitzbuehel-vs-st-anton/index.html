Kitzbühel vs St. Anton

Disclosure: this page contains affiliate links (Booking.com, GetYourGuide). If you book through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It does not change what we recommend. Pages are drafted with AI assistance and checked against official sources; see how we work.

Two of Austria's best-known resorts: a beautiful, upscale Tyrolean town with a famous race course, and a big-mountain, off-piste and après-ski legend on the Arlberg.

Short verdict. Choose St. Anton for the bigger, more challenging ski area, off-piste, and the livelier après-ski. Choose Kitzbühel for a prettier town, good intermediate skiing, and a lower lift-pass price.

Terrain

St. Anton belongs to Ski Arlberg, which officially counts 300 km of marked slopes, 85 lifts and 200 km of off-piste, and links six more villages including Lech and Zürs. It is the bigger and more demanding of the two. Kitzbühel's 233 km and 58 lifts are well suited to confident intermediates, and the KitzSkiWelt Tour adds an 88 km circuit into the neighbouring SkiWelt area. The Hahnenkamm race course is Kitzbühel's famous attraction.

Snow

Kitzbühel sits much lower, and the resort relies heavily on snowmaking: it lists over 1,260 snow guns, and its first skiing weekend is 21–22 November, with later openings depending on conditions. St. Anton is higher and is widely known as one of the snowiest resorts in the Alps, so it is the safer bet for snow, especially in January and March. These altitude and snowfall comparisons are general knowledge rather than figures from the official pages.

Price

Kitzbühel is cheaper on the 2026-27 six-day adult pass: €351–423 depending on dates, against €395–468 for Ski Arlberg. In peak periods the gap is about €45. Kitzbühel's cheapest passes are in early and late season.

Après-ski and village

Kitzbühel is a medieval-style town with upscale shops, restaurants and hotels. St. Anton is a livelier, ski-focused village where the mountain bars are a big part of the evening.

Who should choose which?

St. Anton if you are an advanced skier or want off-piste, a big linked area, reliable snow and a lively après scene.
Kitzbühel if you are an intermediate skier who values a beautiful town and a lower lift-pass price, and you are flexible about conditions early or late in the season.
