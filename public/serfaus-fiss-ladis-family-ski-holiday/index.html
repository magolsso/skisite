Step 2: Choose how to get there

Innsbruck is the closest and most convenient airport, about 93 km and 75 minutes by road. Serfaus has no train station, so most families fly and take a transfer.

Fly to Innsbruck + private transfer

About 75 minutes by road. Book a transfer in advance, since reaching the resort without one is far from easy.

Fly to Munich, Zurich or Friedrichshafen

Roughly 145 to 185 minutes by road (Friedrichshafen about 145, Zurich and Munich about 180 to 185). Good if flights are cheaper.

Train + bus

Nearest station is Landeck-Zams, about an hour by bus from Serfaus. The bus runs infrequently and stops in the early evening, so plan a taxi as a backup.

Step 3: Lessons and the right slopes

Group lessons start at age 3. Both ski schools fill up in peak weeks, so book early. Children born 2021 or later travel free on the lifts; our children aged 7 and 9 need their own pass.

Frequently asked questions

Which village is best for a family ski holiday in Serfaus-Fiss-Ladis?

Serfaus suits under-5s because the car-free village has a free underground train to the lifts. Fiss suits children aged about 5 to 10 and sits next to Berta's Kinderland. Ladis is the quieter, usually cheaper option.

How old must a child be for ski lessons?

Group lessons at the local ski schools start at age 3. Both schools fill up in peak weeks, so book several weeks ahead.

Do children ski free in Serfaus-Fiss-Ladis?

Children born 2021 or later are carried free of charge. Children born 2012 to 2020 pay the child price, and a photo ID is required.

What is the SFL guest card?

A card issued by your accommodation from the first overnight stay in Serfaus, Fiss or Ladis. It gives reduced ski pass prices.

What is the best airport for Serfaus-Fiss-Ladis?

Innsbruck, about 93 km and 75 minutes by road. Friedrichshafen, Munich and Zurich are alternatives at roughly 145 to 185 minutes.

Is there a train to Serfaus?

No. The nearest station is Landeck-Zams, about an hour by bus from Serfaus, with limited evening service.
