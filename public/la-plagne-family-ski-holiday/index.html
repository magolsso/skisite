Step 2: Choose how to get there

Geneva, Chambéry and Lyon are the main airports, and the valley is also reached by train via Aime-La Plagne or Bourg-Saint-Maurice. Transfer times vary with traffic and season, so check your provider's quoted time.

Fly + shared or private transfer

The usual choice. Shared transfers from Geneva start from about €47 per person; compare shared and private options for your dates.

Train

The nearest stations are Aime-La Plagne and Bourg-Saint-Maurice, with a bus up to the resort. Check seasonal direct services from Paris and London before relying on them.

Hire car

Handy for luggage and flexibility. Winter tyres or chains are required, and there are plenty of car parks in the resort.

Step 3: Lessons and the right slopes

Most ski schools take children from age 3, and Piou Piou clubs cover ages 3 to 4 with crèche facilities. Children over 5 need their own pass, skis, helmet and goggles; under-5s need only a hands-free lift card.

What the lift pass costs a family

  • Under 5: free (with a hands-free card for the turnstiles).
  • Ages 5 to 12: a reduced youth rate.
  • Family pass: offered for groups of four or more.
  • Cheaper options: beginner-only passes, half-day tickets and pedestrian passes.

Lift pass prices are published on the official La Plagne price page. Check the Paradiski option if you want to ski Les Arcs as well.

Rest days and wet afternoons

Childcare is easy to find in winter, with crèches, kids' clubs and ski-school lunch clubs for children from a few months to about 12 years. Book nannies and clubs early in busy weeks. For an overview of the area, see our La Plagne ski resort guide and our guide to the best ski resorts in France.

Frequently asked questions

Which village in La Plagne is best for families?

Belle Plagne is peaceful with easy slopes and many ski-in/ski-out options. Plagne Centre is a hub for gentle slopes. Montchavin-Les Coches is quieter. Aime-La Plagne is the budget option but further from the main ski area.

At what age can children start ski lessons in La Plagne?

Most ski schools take children from age 3. Piou Piou clubs cover ages 3 to 4.

Do children need a lift pass in La Plagne?

Children under five go free. Ages 5 to 12 pay a reduced youth rate, and a family pass is offered for groups of four or more.

Where are the best beginner slopes in La Plagne?

Plagne Centre, Plagne Bellecôte and Belle Plagne have dedicated beginner zones, and Montalbert has a children's snow zone at 1,800 m.

How do I get to La Plagne without a car?

Take a flight to Geneva, Chambéry or Lyon and a shared transfer, or take the train to Aime-La Plagne or Bourg-Saint-Maurice and a bus to the resort.

When is La Plagne open in winter 2026/27?

The ski area is open from 12 December 2026 to 24 April 2027.
