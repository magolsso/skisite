Step 1: Choose your base

Where you sleep matters more with children than anywhere else, because everyone is walking in ski boots.

Step 2: Choose how to get there

Chamonix is about an hour by road from Geneva airport.

Fly + shared shuttle

Cheapest door-to-door option. Shuttles such as Alpybus start from about €19.50 per seat, and baby and booster seats are free.

Train from Paris

TGV to Saint-Gervais-Le Fayet, then the local Mont-Blanc Express along the valley. No car needed once you arrive.

Hire car or private transfer

Best with lots of luggage and tired children. You will not need a car in the valley itself.

Step 3: Lessons and the right slopes

Group lessons usually start at age 3, with English-speaking instructors. A half-day lesson is plenty for beginners. Children aged 5 and over need their own lift pass, boots and skis; under-fives need only a hands-free lift card.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best place to stay in Chamonix with young children?

Les Houches is the family favourite: wide, gentle beginner runs, some ski-in/ski-out chalets, and it is closer to Geneva than Chamonix town. Le Tour is quieter with a large beginner area. Chamonix centre suits families who want restaurants and things to do away from the slopes.

Is there a good place for kids to learn to ski in Chamonix?

Yes. The valley has dedicated beginner areas, including La Vormaine at Le Tour, Clos du Savoy in Chamonix centre, Domaine des Planards, and the Tourchet area in Les Houches. Ski lessons usually start around age 3, and half-day lessons are enough for most beginners.

Do children need a lift pass in Chamonix?

Children under 5 ski free with ID. Ages 5 to 14 pay a reduced rate, and family packs for 2 adults and 2 children can cut the price by 10 to 25 percent. Beginners can often use cheaper beginner-area passes.

Does the Chamonix Le Pass cover Les Houches?

No. Le Pass covers the Chamonix valley ski areas but not Les Houches. The Mont-Blanc Unlimited pass covers both, and Les Houches also sells its own lift passes.

How do I get from Geneva airport to Chamonix?

Chamonix is about an hour by road from Geneva airport. Shared shuttles such as Alpybus start from around 19.50 euros per seat, and private transfers and hire cars are the other options. If you are coming from Paris, take the TGV to Saint-Gervais-Le Fayet and continue on the local Mont-Blanc Express train.
